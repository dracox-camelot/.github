## 为什么会有这个组织

我不是先想好要做点什么，再给它起名字的。

是一个晚上，我在 `Dracox-Mao` 那边写完当天最后一笔提交，看着自己散落在四五个仓库里的东西——插件、脚本、试验品、写了一半的文档——忽然觉得它们不像作品，更像**一堆散件**。修得好，能跑，但彼此不认识，也不通向任何地方。

我需要一个地方，让这些散件变成**同一件事的一部分**。

所以有了 dracox-camelot。

它不是一个团队，不是一个产品线，也不是什么"前端框架"式的宏大叙事。它是一个**容器**：把 Dracox 这个名字下所有"还在路上、还没长成"的东西收进来，给它们一条共同的城墙。仓库会很少，提交会很慢，因为这里放的都是要留十年的东西，不是要上热榜的东西。

一句话：**`Dracox-Mao` 是工位，`dracox-camelot` 是城。**

---

## 为什么叫 Camelot

因为我喜欢 Fate。

不是那种"喜欢一个作品"的喜欢，是那种——次次写到深夜，忽然发现自己在用它的句法思考问题的喜欢。

Camelot 在 Fate 里不是一座快乐的城。它是**已经注定要崩塌的、仍然被建成的那座城**。亚瑟王知道结局，还是把剑从石中拔了出来；骑士们知道终局，还是在圆桌旁坐下。整个故事最动人的地方不是那场战争，而是**"明知不行，仍要建城"这个动作本身**。

这正是我做东西的处境。

我写的东西不会成为爆款，我的脚本不会进什么榜单，我修的这些轮子大概一辈子只在几台机器上跑。但我想把它们建得**规整、干净、有名字、有坐标**——像一位明知王国会散、仍然把砖一块块码正的骑士。

所以我不叫它 `Dracox-Labs`、`Dracox-HQ`，或者任何带"未来"的词。我叫它 Camelot：

> 不是因为它辉煌，而是因为它**注定看不到自己的结局**，却依然建了起来。

至于为什么带 `dracox`——那是我的名字。`Dr` 是我在键盘上最先学会的两笔，`a` 是唯一一个只用一根手指就能写完的字母，`x` 是变量，是不确定的那个东西。名字本身就是"一个尚未解出的未知数"。

`-camelot` 是给它的一处**封地**，也是给它的一句判决。

## Why this organization exists

I didn't decide what to build first and name it afterwards.

One night, after pushing the last commit of the day over in `Dracox-Mao`, I looked at what had accumulated across four or five repositories — plugins, scripts, experiments, half-written documents — and realised they didn't read like work. They read like **loose parts**. They ran. But they didn't know each other, and none of them led anywhere.

I needed a place where loose parts could become **parts of the same thing**.

That place is dracox-camelot.

It is not a team, not a product line, not a grand narrative of the "next big framework" kind. It is a **container**: everything under the name Dracox that is still in transit and not yet finished goes in here, and gets one shared wall around it. There will be few repositories and slow commits, because what belongs here is meant to last ten years, not to trend for ten hours.

One line: **`Dracox-Mao` is the desk. `dracox-camelot` is the city.**

---

## Why "Camelot"

Because I love Fate.

Not the ordinary kind of loving a work, but the kind where it's late again, and I notice I've been thinking in its grammar for hours.

Camelot in Fate is not a happy city. It is **the city that was always going to fall, and was built anyway**. Arthur knew the ending and still drew the sword from the stone; the knights knew the ending and still took their seats at the round table. The most moving thing in that story isn't the war. It is **the act of building the city while knowing full well that it cannot hold**.

That is exactly my situation when I make things.

What I write won't go viral. My scripts won't chart. The wheels I fix will probably run on three machines and nowhere else. But I want them built **with order, with cleanliness, with a name and with a coordinate** — like a knight who knows the kingdom will scatter and still lays every brick true.

So it isn't `Dracox-Labs`, or `Dracox-HQ`, or anything carrying the word "future". It is Camelot:

> Not because it is glorious, but because it is **destined never to see its own ending** — and was raised regardless.

As for the `dracox` part: it's my name. `Dr` were the first two keys I ever learned to find, `a` is the only letter one finger can finish on its own, and `x` is the variable — the thing still undetermined. The name itself is *an unknown not yet solved*.

`-camelot` is the **fief** granted to it, and also its **sentence**.

## What will grow here

Honestly: not much yet.

| Status | Detail |
|---|---|
| Repositories | Starting from zero, moving things in one at a time |
| Pace | Slow. One thing at a time — and everything admitted must come with a wall |
| Rule | It needs a `README`, a description, and to be readable by a stranger — or it doesn't get in |
| Not done here | No demo repos, no "practice collection", no opening without a continuation |

If you pass by one day and see only two or three repositories, don't conclude anything yet. **A city starts with a single stone.**
